retained Laurent polynomial. Version 1 stopped during preparation on two rustfmt rewrites in the recovery
check, before compilation. The original inputs, failure and generated sources
are preserved. Version 2 records the corrected formatting expectations before
execution and requires its three source maps to match the formatted version 1
maps exactly. No mathematical or test change is involved; no runtime is admitted.

Three fresh sources distinguish the unchanged endpoint/P4 baseline, the
previously rejected recursive-view control, and that control with direct
projection. The [scope](scope-v2.json) binds the source review and fixed cost
protocol. Earlier samples remain closed. This comparison neither admits the
recursive control nor attributes its earlier timing regressions to copying.

The projected variant borrows coefficient slices through a private read-only
trait and clones only the exact rational components returned to callers.
Rational zero, pi-power matching, quadratic shape checks and opaque rejection
keep their original meaning. The compacting consumer still materializes an
owned polynomial before reconstruction. Successful rational queries retain
their original sign publication, and quadratic dispatch tracing remains after
successful projection.

The existing owned/retained result enum, graph-free closed payload, recursive
arithmetic, memo insertion and ownership remain. The original retry follows a
failed reused-certificate pass after releasing its memo. No recognition pass,
cache fact, approximation, dependency, public API or budget is added. P4 permits
stronger cached facts, never contradictions or weaker available facts.
