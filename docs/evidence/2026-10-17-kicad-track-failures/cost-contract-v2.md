# Cost and artifact contract after the coincident-track correction

Retain cost-contract-v1's complete measurements, timing boundaries, alternating
pairs, raw reports, original workload gates, external containment and limits
on claims. Contract-v4 deliberately changes the geometry of formerly omitted
coincident tracks; the first comparison contract's identical-output requirement
therefore applies to unchanged features and unaffected fixtures.

Before measurement, identify all such tracks independently from the complete
source files with Python Fraction arithmetic. A successful candidate must emit
every positive-width segment. Its added features must correspond exactly to
those source disks; removing precisely those declared additions from its
observed sequence must reproduce the full baseline metadata and finite report
coordinate bits. All other features, drills, outlines and panel output remain
identical. Keep both complete representations and every delta.

Separately verify each added disk in the complete board against exact center,
radius, area πr², point membership, and native bounds, under strict→approximate
→strict. These mathematical checks are distinct from report-byte comparison.

Only fixtures with no geometric additions are matched completed-contract cost
comparisons. For a fixture with added disks, record both full loads and their
costs, but identify the changed workload and claim no speedup or like-for-like
ratio. Both original full-board and copper-neck tests must still pass under
their unchanged policies, resolutions and limits.

Retain every raw CLI report difference. Version 20→21 is expected; any other
change must be individually explained from the declared geometry correction
before admission. Do not silently strip findings, metadata, or failed cases.
