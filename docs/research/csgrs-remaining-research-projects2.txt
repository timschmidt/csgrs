EXECUTIVE ASSESSMENT

The campaign closed or narrowed most original leads. Its ledger records 14
accepted, 12 accepted in part, 7 deferred, 6 rejected, 3 not applicable and
2 rejected with a different replacement accepted. These are 44 dispositions,
not 44 independently delivered features. The right next campaign is smaller:
finish exact continuation and address measured costs at their actual owners.
In parallel, retain and test promising advanced API alternatives under the
experimental charter below, rather than repeat unchanged rejected variants.

Important accepted work includes cold Real-domain validation, bounded optional
mesh coordinate sharing, approximate embedded-complex qualification, retained
mesh incidence, compact exact intersection coordinates, local selected-root
comparison, integer polynomial refinement and remainder kernels, rational-line
polynomial sections, exact line-profile modeling, monotone fixed-plane sweeps,
box containment, shared cap/wall assembly, dependency pruning and size harnesses.
The campaign's negative results are valuable constraints. In particular, the
failed modular GCD and modular radical proposals targeted costs that turned out
to be exact zero remainders and rational refinement, respectively.

The main remaining risks are repeated exact closure: local fillet-center
creation, tangency after reconstruction, polynomial-family continuation,
terminal-qualified consumers, mixed exact/sampled profiles and revolutions.
New source analysis also identifies an odd-segment full-turn revolution contract gap:
adding a disjoint component can change an existing component's sampled geometry.
Both phase conventions are documented/tested; resolving their composition needs an
independent production regression and an explicit semantic migration decision.

Priorities: exactness > completeness and operation success > performance > low
memory > binary size > source size. Hypercurve has special priority. A private
helper with a proved narrow contract is a useful baseline, not a permanent API
constraint. Compare advanced alternatives for semantic reach, proof reuse and
composition as well as implementation cost; fewer source lines alone are insufficient.

MAINTAINER DECISIONS AND EXPERIMENTAL API CHARTER

These decisions supersede conflicting future-work gates below; they do not
rewrite the campaign's measured results or its 44 historical dispositions.
The library remains experimental, near-green-field computational geometry
research. Significant API changes are expected and the best API is not yet
known. The aim is to get the most from the least. Record potential API
improvements and test advanced implementations, not just superficial wrappers.
No current caller, a public API break, or lack of a stability commitment is by
itself grounds to discard a promising design. Existing callers remain useful
controls, not the only legitimate source of future requirements. Most from
least means capability, invariants and proof reuse per concept, not simply
the smallest number of public types. No justification to adopt yet does not
mean do not investigate.

An exploratory entry must name an advanced capability or simplification,
a concrete implementation hypothesis, a discriminating fixture family and a
decision gate. A designed future CAD/CAM workload may supply that gate; label
it as exploratory rather than pretend it is observed production demand.
Compare against the simplest adequate baseline, preserving exactness, domain,
source authority, policy, ownership and continuation. Record negative results
for the tested variant/workload without declaring the abstract design space
closed. Do not mechanically rerun rejected code under a new name. Adoption
needs demonstrated semantic/compositional value or measured cost reduction,
with tradeoffs reported in the unchanged priority order. Source/API migrations
are expected research outcomes and should be explicit, not automatic vetoes.

- Geometry versus parameterization: distinguish geometric-equivalence
  operations from parameter-preserving operations explicitly. Reparameterizing
  or resegmenting may be valid when exact oriented geometry, topology and
  required source correspondence are preserved. Preserve traversal where
  meaningful; retain or prove correspondence when a consumer depends on
  parameters. Authored segment IDs are not silently universal invariants,
  and geometric equality does not establish parameter equivalence.
- Approximate ancestry: allowed operations may proceed under explicit policy
  with qualifications preserved. A later operation may certify its result on
  the represented geometry; it must not erase approximate construction or
  source ancestry. Keep this independent from current-operation certainty.
- Revolutions: meshes should preserve authored meridians and ease OpenSCAD
  compatibility; for the same authored profile/start, canonicalize repeated
  full-turn sweep coverage without discarding the meridian, and support integer
  full-turn multiples. This does not equate odd-N meshes from positive and
  negative radial profiles. BRep must keep geometric and parametric information
  while using canonical geometric support identity for equivalent full revolutions. R01 separates this target from verified
  reference-version behavior; reference saturation of >360 is not evidence of
  a parameter-preserving multi-turn API. Oversized noninteger sweeps need a
  separate decision rather than automatic adoption of reference quirks.
- Archive/API stability: long-term format compatibility and standardization
  are deferred until the API and structure stabilize. Current cold decode,
  exact-domain validation, proof/trust boundaries and same-version continuation
  remain correctness obligations. Record the producing schema/version and
  intentional migrations; do not promise permanent schemas or backward
  compatibility across experimental revisions.
- Targets: web, desktop, workstation and supercomputer; browser WASM is likely
  the tightest constraint. CAD/CAM workflows matter most. Push exact geometric
  computation toward competitiveness with f64 plus degeneracy handling, using
  semantically matched workloads and honest failure/robustness accounting.
  This does not change exactness > completeness and operation success >
  performance > low memory > binary size > source size, or Hypercurve priority.
  The prior embedded-z decision remains in force for that profile; the current
  target matrix is the web/desktop/workstation/supercomputer set above.
- Accepted starting workflow gates: (1) arc/line fillet -> rebuild -> reverse
  -> refillet -> erosion; (2) NURBS fillet -> round stroke; (3) mixed
  curved/linear enclosure modeling with tiny exact features and repeated cuts.
  These are end-to-end acceptance gates, not claims they already pass or a
  substitute for the boundary/adverse controls in R02/R03/R04/R25.
- ABC Models Archive: the maintainer is arranging a separate exhaustive corpus
  effort, overseen and reported by another agent that will file stack bugs.
  Do not take over or contact that effort. When its reports are available,
  R25 should integrate minimized, provenance-rich regressions and link them
  to the originating model/report; corpus ownership remains separate.
- S01 remains explicitly NOT ADOPTED FOR NOW. This general permission to
  explore APIs is not a reversal of that specific maintainer decision.

MATHEMATICAL AND RESOURCE CONTRACTS

- Fixed-field linear constructions need not enlarge the field, but coefficient
  heights, DAG size and exact predicate costs can still grow without bound.
- Preserve selected roots, source relations, chart/domain, branch, denominator,
  orientation and multiplicity authority. A local presentation does not reduce
  a genuinely large absolute algebraic degree. The hard fillet center's selected
  factor is degree 164, not an assumed degree 214 and not a small-degree value.
- Structural equality can prove equality; structural inequality cannot prove
  inequality. Shared support membership alone does not select an intersection.
  A common factor with overlapping brackets does not prove the same selected root.
- General computable-real equality is not universally decidable. Unsupported,
  undecided, invalid, exhausted/cancelled, empty and unbounded remain distinct.
- Preserve exact successful routes and fallbacks. A fitted curve, tessellation or
  display is not an exact substitute for its analytic or algebraic source.
- Optional optimization may decline; hard enclosing resource exhaustion may not
  be hidden by resetting/refunding work or starting an uncharged fallback.
  Preserve each API's documented cancellation and cached-proof precedence.
- Keep inherited construction fidelity separate from certainty consumed by the
  current operation. Exact arithmetic on rounded geometry does not certify the
  original source. A strict later call cannot erase approximate ancestry.
- Keep open incidence, regularized filled sets and manifold conversion separate.
  Record intentional row/schema/API migrations without assuming permanent
  experimental-format compatibility; do not infer equal sets from equal volume,
  triangle counts or successful rendering alone.

R01 Make segmented revolution independent of unrelated profile components
Priority: P0 exactness. Evidence: Source-derived contract inconsistency; production regression unrun.
Objective: Preserve authored signed meridians in mesh revolution while canonicalizing full-turn
sweep coverage consistently, including integer multiples, negative radial profiles and odd segment
counts. Separate BRep canonical geometric support identity from retained parametric information.
Current evidence: C04 added an analytic absolute-radius fold only when the full profile has both
radial signs. For three angular segments, N=[-2,-1] x [0,1] alone has x bounds [-2,1]. Adding the
vertically disjoint P=[1,2] x [2,3] triggers folding, and N then has x bounds [-1,2]. The exact
source-lattice model also changes membership of (-3/2,1/2,0). A five-segment example behaves
similarly. Negative-only tests preserve authored slice vertices, while mixed-sign tests compare to a
hand-folded profile. Both conventions and one-sided compatibility were intentional and documented;
their composition is inconsistent under a component-local sampled-set contract. This is not a Rust
runtime reproduction or an undisclosed wrong-value allegation.
Maintainer direction: Keep the authored meridian/phase for meshes; canonicalize repeated full-turn
sweep coverage, not that phase. Preserve authored profile, start, sign, turn count and parameter map
where relevant. For BRep, equivalent full revolutions should share canonical geometric support
identity while retaining their parametric information and distinct topological uses. Geometrically
equal analytic support does not imply identical finite odd-segment meshes. Integer multiples are
in scope; oversized noninteger sweeps remain an explicit semantic decision gate.
Verified reference facts (source inspection only, no OpenSCAD execution): Current upstream
a3bf46a2b6d6597eb947567515f382fe07fd042d (2026-10-24) maps signed radius directly to
(r cos(theta), r sin(theta), y), retaining the negative-radius pi phase. It rejects global mixed
radial signs across outlines rather than folding them. Angle zero is empty. Finite angle >360 or
<=-360 saturates to +360, including 720, -720 and nonmultiples such as 450 and -1000; this is not
modulo reduction or preservation of authored turns. With explicit finite angle, omitted start
defaults to 0; with angle omitted, omitted start defaults to 180. Explicit start is honored for a
full turn. Stable 2021.01 (41f58fe57c03457a3a8b4dc541ef5654ec3e8c78) instead uses phase 180 for
full turns, has no start parameter, and uses phase 0 for partial turns. PR 5553 records a deliberate
phase change. Thus compatibility fixtures must pin reference version and call syntax. These are
reference observations, not a decision to copy every reference quirk into CSGRS.
Implementation experiment: Add a public-API regression comparing the negative component alone and as
a disjoint union. Preserve its authored phase; test phase-aware regularized solid union for mixed
radial components or explicitly specify another CSGRS extension. OpenSCAD rejects that mixed-sign
domain, so do not label a broader CSGRS rule reference-compatible behavior. Define canonical full-
turn coverage independently of authored meridian and parameter metadata; do not let an unrelated
component select the discretization convention. Compare BRep support identity separately from
parameter/topological-use records. Record any intended migration of existing rows. Choose and
document the noninteger oversized-angle rule only after comparing the desired CAD/CAM semantics.
Mathematical guardrails: For even N, a pi shift permutes the angular lattice; for odd N it does not.
Analytic circles and finite regular polygons are not interchangeable. Full-turn folding is
analytically valid but need not preserve the same sampled set.
Acceptance: Exact membership and bounds for N=3,5 and even controls (including N=6); angles
+/-90, +/-360, +/-720 and 1080; explicit start 37 versus omitted start/angle; signed radial
reflection, mixed/one-sided profiles and axis contact. Include 450 and values immediately either
side of the 360/-360 policy boundaries; their CSGRS expected results depend on the explicit
noninteger-sweep decision, not a guessed modulo rule. Cover holes, overlapping/disjoint heights,
operand/component order and repeated revolution-to-Boolean continuation. Check orientation, closure
and winding, not merely volume. For BRep, test equal geometric support with retained authored
start/sign/turn count and parameter correspondence; do not require equal odd-N mesh sets solely
because support is equal. Pin OpenSCAD version and exact call syntax in compatibility fixtures.
Retain the previous overcount regression separately.
Old ID mapping: C04; new discretization invariant beyond the accepted analytic fold.

ADVANCED EXPLORATORY API REGISTER

These are bounded research alternatives, not extra project IDs or promises of
adoption. Serious implementation prototypes may use synthetic or anticipated
CAD/CAM geometry without a current production caller. Keep the campaign's
negative controls and distinguish rejected variants from the larger design
space. Each experiment must expose exactness/domain, policy/cancellation,
ownership and continuation consequences; evaluate cost in the existing
priority order. No proposal here reopens S01 without a new maintainer decision.

H01 Restricted support and chart view
Hypothesis: A source-bound restriction/map view can carry selected branch,
parameter interval, orientation and correlated endpoints once, so advanced
mapped/polynomial consumers reuse authority rather than rebuild family proofs.
Prototype native/retained/restricted representations through split, reverse,
map and recut. Gate: same admitted geometry and required correspondence with
fewer independent proof obligations or broader exact continuation, measured
against narrow per-family services; no generic view accepted merely for fewer lines.

H02 Advanced pair capability API
Hypothesis: An explicit capability-driven pair-event kernel can add selected-
coefficient and mapped polynomial families without a Cartesian explosion of
family dispatch or loss of common-component authority. Implement one demanding
anticipated pair family, not a placeholder universal trait. Gate: complete
contacts/components and both-operand split/reintersect, independent oracle,
honest typed unsupported cases, and an evidenced extensibility/cost comparison.

H03 Compositional relation and contact algebra
Hypothesis: A relation result retaining overlap intervals, isolated contacts,
multiplicity and chart correspondence can compose through restrictions and
noninjective maps without dropping residual events. Test overlap-plus-tangency,
retracing, multiple roots and endpoint contacts beyond the already passing
singleton cases. Gate: complete event replay and no false equivalence or erased
contact, with a demonstrable reduction in duplicated semantic rules.

H09 Local source-union and selected-field joins
Hypothesis: A demand-driven joined-source authority can support independent
selected/mapped inputs while delaying unnecessary global coordinate promotion;
retain algebraic dependencies rather than treating correlated sources as free.
Prototype future branch-pair/fillet fixtures plus the retained synthetic gap.
Gate: exact selected solution and denominator/branch checks, source-drop replay,
relative/absolute degree and memory/time accounting; reject only this variant
if no capability/value emerges, not all possible joins for lack of current hits.

H16 Adaptive elevation and preparation API
Hypothesis: A demand-aware preparation plan can choose sequential elevation for
nearby degrees and direct/checkpoint work for sparse high-degree requests,
sharing only certified reusable data. Test sequential degrees, sparse jumps,
low-degree controls and anticipated high-order CAD/CAM spans with cold/warm/drop
states. Gate: semantic parity and a useful capability or cost envelope without
concealing the prior sequential regression or unbounded retained memory.

H17 Demand-driven arrangement and selection view
Hypothesis: A proof-bearing arrangement view can evaluate only requested output
selections and reuse output-relevant certification across later requests, rather
than share the already-small all-four stage. Prototype repeated/subset selections
with retained source lineage and downstream reconstruction. Gate: complete exact
outputs, honest policy/receipt reuse and worthwhile avoided work versus both the
old all-four design and R11-R13/R28's narrow baselines; count retention and teardown.

AffineMap2 and RegionPointLocation
Hypothesis: A coherent map/classification API can preserve chart, orientation,
location witness and policy across affine pullback, instead of adding synonyms
for existing wrappers. Prototype nonuniform scale/shear/reflection, inverse and
composition on an advanced admitted family; classify then split/Boolean/recut.
Gate: exact composition and boundary semantics, explicit singular-map exclusions,
fewer repeated consumer contracts or broader success, and an explicit API migration.

Certainty unification without semantic conflation
Hypothesis: A common result/provenance algebra with independent construction-
fidelity, operation-certainty and policy axes can simplify composition while
retaining each domain's meanings. Prototype map/and_then, explicit-policy queries
and strict/approximate/strict continuation. Gate: no certification upgrade or
ancestry loss; preserve invalid/unsupported/undecided/exhausted distinctions,
error/cancellation precedence and receipts. Compare a shared semantic model with
both adapters and current enums; one flattened enum is not the research goal.

Wrapper naming and minimal usable operation surface
Hypothesis: A consistent fallibility/policy vocabulary can make advanced chains
expressible with fewer conceptual exceptions without hiding errors or mandatory
approximation. Prototype representative fillet/Boolean/mesh/BRep chains through
an actually usable alternate surface, not only a name list. Gate: callers can
identify geometry, defaults, fallibility and receipts from the API; exact domain,
diagnostics, cancellation and construction fidelity stay explicit. Public breaks
are allowed with migration examples. Do not count renaming as a runtime win or
silently redefine sampled circle geometry. Distinct diagnostic contracts need not
collapse merely to minimize wrapper/type count.
