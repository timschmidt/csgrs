## Retention Policy

- Every registered fuzz target contributes deterministic replay seeds.
- Each refresh releases the fastest promoted case and promotes the worst eligible historical offender.
- `promoted_slow_offenders.txt` is the durable 100-case replay set used for dedicated Criterion rows and the lexicase score.
