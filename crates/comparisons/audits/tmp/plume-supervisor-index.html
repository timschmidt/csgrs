I am a lecturer at the School of Computer Science of the University of Birmingham. My first degree was from the Universidade Federal do Rio Grande Sul in Brazil, where I also obtained an MSc degree by research at the same institution. During my undergraduate and MSc studies, I worked in industry. I then went to Imperial College of the University of London for my PhD. After completing this, I was a postdoc for one year at Imperial, a lecturer at the University of Edinburgh for two years, and then at the University of St Andrews for one year.
My current research interests include continuous lattices in analysis and topology, effective real analysis, topological methods in the theory of computation, locale theory, programming language semantics, domain theory, lambda-calculus, functional programming.
