Description

template<class X>
class Ariadne::FirstDifferential< X >

A class representing the partial derivatives of a scalar quantity depending on multiple arguments.

Based on a power series Expansion, centred on the point at which the partial derivatives are being evaluated.

Invariant
The expansion is sorted using graded_sort(). In particular, the total degree of the terms is increasing, and the linear terms appear in coordinate order.
