 Solve \(\dt{\phi}(x,t)=f(\phi(x,t))\) for \(x\in D\) and \(t\in[0,h]\), assuming that the flow remains in \(B\). If the flow does not remain in \(B\), then \(\hat{\phi}(x,t)\) may not be a bound for \(\phi(x,t)\) if \(\exists \tau\in[0,t],\ \phi(x,\tau)\not\in B\). If \((h,B)\) have been computed using the flow_bounds() method, then the flow is guarenteed to be correct for all \(x\in D\) and \(t\in[0,h]\).
Returns: A validated version \(\hat{\phi}\) of the flow over a short step represented as a single function over a box.
Arguments: \(f\) is the vector_field, \(D\) is the state_domain, \(h\) is the time_step, and \(B\) is the state_bounding_box.
Throws: A FlowTimeStepException if the flow cannot be computed sufficiently accurately for the given time step.
