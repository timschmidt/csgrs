\doxysubsubsubsubsection{Description}
Classes for representing discretised sets. 

A {\itshape subdivision tree} is a binary tree describing a subdivision of a rectangle ({\itshape unit cell}) into smaller cells. The subdivision is described by a Subdivision\+Sequence giving the coordinate of the ith subdivision, and a Binary\+Tree giving the subdivisions.

e.\+g. The cell described by the Subdivision\+Sequence \textquotesingle{}\mbox{[}0,0,1,...\mbox{]}\textquotesingle{} and the \doxylink{classAriadne_1_1BinaryWord}{Binary\+Word} \textquotesingle{}\mbox{[}0,1,1\mbox{]}\textquotesingle{} is given by subdividing the unit interval twice in the $x_0$ direction, first taking the lower half, and then the upper half, and by subdividing once in the $x_1$ direction, taking the upper half. This gives the dyadic rectangle \textquotesingle{}\mbox{[}0.\+25,0.\+5\mbox{]}x\mbox{[}0.\+5,1.\+0\mbox{]}\textquotesingle{}.

Denotable sets are unions of basic sets of a simple kind, typically boxes. In addition to the fundamental geometric predicates, denotable sets must also support iteration through their elements, and union (join) with basic sets and denotable sets of the same kind. \doxysubsubsubsubsubsection*{Classes}
