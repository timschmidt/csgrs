\doxysubsubsection{Description}
A zonotope of arbitrary dimension. 

A zonotope is a set of the form $c+Ge$, where $||e||_{\infty}\leq1$. The columns of the matrix $G$ are the {\itshape generators} of the zonotope.

Zonotopes are always is\+\_\+bounded. A zonotope always contains its centre point, so can never be empty. However, it may not be regular.

The intersection and membership tests may be performed using algorithms from\+: ~\newline
 Guibas, Leonidas J.; Nguyen, An; Zhang, Li, "{}\+Zonotopes as bounding volumes."{} {\itshape Proceedings of the Fourteenth Annual ACM-\/\+SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms} (Baltimore, MD, 2003), 803--812, ACM, New York, 2003.

{\bfseries{\doxylink{classAriadne_1_1Storage}{Storage}\+:}} A Zonotope in dimension d with n generators is described by d(n+1) real numbers.
