class Ariadne\+::\+First\+Differential$<$ X $>$}
A class representing the partial derivatives of a scalar quantity depending on multiple arguments. 

Based on a power series \doxylink{classAriadne_1_1Expansion}{Expansion}, centred on the point at which the partial derivatives are being evaluated.

\begin{DoxyInvariant}{Invariant}
The expansion is sorted using graded\+\_\+sort(). In particular, the total degree of the terms is increasing, and the linear terms appear in coordinate order. 
\end{DoxyInvariant}
