Share exact derivative images across polynomial and rational Beziers

Native quadratic and cubic derivatives now use the existing rational-function
image carrier with a unit denominator and selected-source polynomial reduction.
This retains arbitrary exact coefficients and repairs eight independently
reproduced derivative/endpoint image failures.

Remove the redundant polynomial coordinate/tangent types and endpoint tangent
enum, update every active caller directly, and store optional shared derivative
handles without extra boxes. Preserve derivative-order demand, rational quotient
recurrence, and parameter-cache ownership rules.

Validation: 321 release cases across 12 pinned executables, the unchanged public
counterexample probe, both Hypercurve Clippy configurations, formatting, four
fuzz-target builds, documentation, Hyperbrep Clippy, and csgrs all-target checks.
The new regression independently checks quadratic/cubic derivatives through
third order for sqrt(2) and pi coefficients under both policies.
