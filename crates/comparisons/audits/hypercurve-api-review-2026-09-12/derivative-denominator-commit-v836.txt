Retain reduced denominator powers in algebraic derivative recurrence

Derivative images already reduce their published denominators at the selected
source root. Reuse those reduced values between multiplications instead of
retaining and expanding the full D^(k+1) polynomial. Keep numerator recurrence
on full source polynomials because those polynomials are differentiated.
Preserve original pole semantics and the existing certified reduction fallback.

The new independent regression checks eight derivative orders across homogeneous
source degrees and a reducible source equation sharing a factor with D: a
finite selected root remains usable while the excluded root remains a pole.

Validation: 324 release cases across 12 executables, seven integration checks,
36 unchanged public probe samples with independent exact-value checks, and
four unchanged finite/pole domain cases. On the degree-15/eight-order probe,
median Rust allocation requests fell from about 133k to 87k and requested
bytes from 8.7MB to 5.8MB under both policies. Timings are allocator-instrumented
and workload-local; no uniform performance claim.
