# Retain finite corner extensions in their source chart (read-only design)

The V31/V32 quartic stacks reached whole-unit self-intersection elimination on a newly reparameterized extension carrier. The integer determinant and sample-history reductions address measured algebraic waste. They do not remove the carrier reconstruction that made old source evidence inapplicable.

`canonicalize_retained_extension_on_finite_envelope` in bezier_region.rs creates a new source, maps the cut parameters, rebuilds both point images, and publishes an analytic/selected replacement with an inverse affine map. Its only production callers are the one- and two-cut canonicalizers in curve_corner_chain.rs. `CurveSupport2::restrict_certified` already expresses the needed result directly: original support, ordered finite range, retained endpoint images, traversal bit.

A coherent next migration should validate the finite range's source denominator, retain the original support and point/parameter identities, and remove the inverse chart fields and tangent reattachment used only by this reconstruction. A replacement fragment may still be needed to communicate the extended range to companion fillet checks; it need not be a fresh curve or require inverse-chart metadata. Keep native control-net subdivision as an optional materialization operation, not as range admission.

Do not merely widen `retained_selected_corner_parameter_is_in_native_chart`: it presently proves [0,1] membership in multiple reconstruction/admissibility paths. The selected extension branch, analytic branch, one-fragment rebuild, companion contact range, and materialized/retained-Bezier reconstruction must migrate together. `CornerReplacement2` is defined in curve.rs; its two retained variants and parallel_source_parameter_map expose the otherwise unnecessary construction history. The map is consumed when reattaching a fillet tangent to a canonical anchor. Those callers must update directly with no identity-map compatibility shim.

The former rational affine subcurve constructor explicitly excludes projective poles over the chosen finite envelope and starts a fresh lineage to prevent unit-only injectivity facts leaking onto tails. A retained-chart implementation must preserve pole exclusion and keep every unit-only certificate domain-guarded. A simple closed unit parallel does not prove its extensions have no further self-contacts. Existing finite self-intersection discovery can operate in the original chart, but completeness and runtime must be measured rather than inferred.

Required evidence includes existing pre-pole rational extension tests, resource-blocked selected exterior endpoints, one-/two-sided edits, reversed traversal, both policies, the nonzero quartic loop, retained-circle fillets, subsequent booleans and offsets. Replace tests asserting the old envelope/map representation with exact geometric/set and retained-identity oracles. No production edits are made in this design note.

## Further caller audit during V38 build

CornerReplacement2 has three variants and roughly thirty local mentions, concentrated in curve.rs, curve_corner_chain.rs and bezier_region.rs. The two generated variants carry an inverse affine map used only by retained fillet anchor tangent reattachment; the plain Curve variant also serves retained circular companion canonicalization, so deleting the enum indiscriminately would remove a distinct current construction route. A unified replacement fragment may eliminate variant rewrapping, but native circular cut parameters still have to match its chart.

The one-sided canonicalizer currently supplies the changed cut point as a placeholder for the retained endpoint because the finite-envelope helper recomputes both images. A source-retaining implementation must instead obtain and preserve the actual untouched endpoint image. Selected companion extension admission must use the replacement's certified finite range; its current native-chart check is not that proof. Existing resource-blocked projective-extension tests assert inverse chart restoration and should be rewritten around unchanged source parameter/point identities and exact range/traversal. CurveSupport2 already guards unit-only injectivity facts by the active range, but self-contact discovery on finite exterior ranges must still be audited and exercised. No production changes made in this audit.
