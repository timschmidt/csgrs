Preserve parameterization invariance in third-order tangent ordering

The local curves (t,t^3) and (2u,8u^3) received different orders despite
tracing one graph. Squared third-order determinants require |v|^8, not
|v|^4, when both curvature witnesses vanish. Correct this normalization
in native and represented arithmetic.

Require both saved curvature signs to be zero before the retained
third-order fallback. Equal nonzero curvature alone does not justify
that rule: (u+a*u^2,(u+a*u^2)^2) describes the same parabola for different
a, while its raw third derivatives differ.

Validate independent cubic graph orders at nine speed pairs under both
policies, native/represented parity and the reparameterized parabola.
The unchanged six-case public probe repairs all four baseline mismatches.
Qualification: 333 selected release tests in 12 pinned binaries, seven
format/lint/fuzz/doc/downstream checks. Evidence is in
hypercurve-api-review-2026-09-12/third-order-speed-v861-reviewed.json.
