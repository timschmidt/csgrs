Fuse exact quadratic arithmetic and reuse signed branches

Use the existing fused rational product sums for quadratic products and norms. Decide signs from equal coefficient signs before constructing a larger norm, and compare opposed rational products without materializing their difference. Preserve principal-root branches, exact cancellation, and general scalar fallback.
