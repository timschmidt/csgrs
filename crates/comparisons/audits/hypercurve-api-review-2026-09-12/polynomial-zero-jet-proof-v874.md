# Degree-certified zero derivative evidence

A polynomial of degree at most d has identically zero derivatives of every order greater than d, for every exact coefficient and every parameter. Native quadratic and cubic source types provide those degree bounds. The retained derivative-source adapter can therefore return an exact zero vector for orders at least 3 and 4 respectively, without projecting the selected parameter or constructing coordinate eliminants. Reversal preserves that zero. Rational curve types are excluded from this rule: their nonconstant denominators can have nonzero derivatives of arbitrarily high order.

For native quadratic endpoint data, the third derivative is retained as zero only when higher derivative evidence is requested. Topology-only and first-order demand remain unchanged. Missing evidence for other source kinds remains missing. All existing derivative construction, rational quotient, finite-domain and scalar/root arithmetic paths are unchanged.

V873 compares (t,t^2) and (u+u^2,u^2), in native quadratic, equal-weight conic and general rational carriers, under both policies. At matching x=t=u+u^2, the first y-coordinate exceeds the second by 2u^3+u^4>0. Thus the second outgoing branch precedes the first from the incoming positive horizontal direction. The probe covers native and retained materialized traversal plus selected algebraic endpoint traversal. All twelve conic/general cases succeed, while all six native quadratic cases block because zero third derivatives were treated as absent. V875 replays the unchanged source and requires all eighteen to succeed.

Regression tests retain that independent graph/carrier oracle and also request derivatives beyond native quadratic/cubic degree at an algebraic parameter, with pi coefficients and both traversal orientations. They require exact zero coordinate witnesses rather than sampled bounds. This migration establishes the demonstrated representation equivalence; it does not prove universal curve-operation closure or a measured performance improvement.
