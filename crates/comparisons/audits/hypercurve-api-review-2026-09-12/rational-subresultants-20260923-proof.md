# Selected-root common-factor certificate

The candidate replaces the private derivative-only integer kernel with one subresultant generator shared by local Sturm counting and common-factor replay. It accepts genuinely rational payloads and uses independent positive rational scales to obtain integer bivariate inputs. General exact Real coefficients and retained quotients keep their field implementation.

For ordered inputs A,B with degree gap d, Brown's first remainder is (-1)^(d+1) prem(A,B). With l=lc(B) and c=-l^d, each later degree gap e uses divisor -l*c^e. The new scale is -lc(next) for e=1, and (-lc(next))^e/c^(e-1) otherwise. Every division is checked by exact division in Z[t]. Pseudo-division completes all nominal leading-coefficient powers, including powers skipped when an elimination cancels more than one term.

The local wrapper owns a separate STRICT proof field. It certifies the first leading coefficient and every retained nonzero row's leading coefficient on the selected real root. Every multiplier and divisor before termination is therefore nonzero at that root. Polynomial identities remain valid under specialization, without assuming the defining polynomial is irreducible. An entire zero row ends the sequence; a nonzero row whose degree drops declines this path. The generator delivers rows as needed and stops immediately on that selected termination. Prior inverse and sign evidence is preserved; the new strict sign certificates and refined singleton are transferred to the caller.
