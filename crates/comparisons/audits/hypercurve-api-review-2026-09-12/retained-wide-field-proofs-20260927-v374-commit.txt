Retain exact quadratic-field proofs across wide coefficients

Remove coefficient-size gates from exact quadratic-field arithmetic, retained
reductions and compaction. Wide intermediate values can cancel exactly and
later scalar queries can reuse their shared rational coefficients instead of
replaying numerical construction histories. Clone a reduction only when the
cache will publish it; add no new public scalar representation.

Keep the explicit binary-shift allocation guard, measuring new expansion after
cancellation rather than rejecting already represented wide coefficients.
Cover wide nested-radical cancellation, tiny signed perturbations, exported
proofs and small shifts; existing huge-shift and concurrent-cache cases pass.

Validation: Hyperreal and Hypersolve unit/integration suites, 232 selected
Hypercurve release tests, all-target Clippy, formatting, editing fuzz, denied-
warning docs and Hyperbrep checks. The two full extended-fillet re-offset
performance cases remain unresolved and are excluded from this qualification.
