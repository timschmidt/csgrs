# Consolidate rational overlap and residual replay

Continuation of the original active architecture goal after `daffa15`. That finite rational-pair implementation is progress; the full goal remains unchanged.

The common support dispatcher now treats the scalar rational context as the fast authority for isolated contacts and certified injective correspondences. Incomplete scalar replay and non-injective shared images enter the complete finite component authority before publication. That authority retains every selected component and residual contact on both active domains. It also covers unit domains with identity charts, so these cases do not allocate affine-chart metadata.

Removed the separate common-dispatch overlap/self-contact replay, its auxiliary blocker conversion, and the `Span::self_contacts` cache. The older route tried to reconstruct off-diagonal contacts through a single correspondence and explicitly blocked partial/non-bijective maps. Consolidation removes roughly 120 lines of that special replay machinery and the per-span cache field; unit provenance paths for injective overlaps remain available. Renamed the internal domain-report constructor from `isolated` to `enumerated` because a fully enumerated result can now include finite overlaps. No alias or compatibility adapter remains.

The regression now requires one complete anti-diagonal overlap and the distinct diagonal endpoint contact. It replays both overlap endpoints and reuses published topology pieces under both policies and operand orders.
