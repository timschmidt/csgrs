# Evaluable region carrier evidence

Committed Hypercurve `7faa8340e1bf79c823e618f78feedc186213eda9` and HyperBREP `878fcb441b281ab1b44051ca4420b28092c3c2ed`. All 30 repositories are clean after commit.

The original goal remains active. This change repairs the public chart-ownership boundary; it does not claim completion of the full Boolean/offset/corner/computational closure plan.

`CurveRegionCarrier2` replaces `CurveRegionCarrierRef2`. Each referenced carrier owns a shared `Curve2` in the exact chart used by its contact/overlap parameters, plus the input operand, loop and fragment provenance. Its index is local to the report. The former separate family field is removed; callers query the actual curve. `CurveRegionBoundaryContact2` retains the same carrier, and its duplicate segment-index accessor is removed. HyperBREP, the UI, tests, benches and fuzz callers are migrated directly. No alias or compatibility adapter remains.

Publication uses the existing support/range restriction machinery. Each report creates a curve only for a carrier that survives into a contact, overlap or blocker, and shares it across those records. No permanent curve field is added to every Boolean carrier. Whole chord/circle fragments reuse their endpoint and tangent evidence by identity. Bezier source restrictions keep their original chart and lazy first-order algebraic endpoint images. The old `AlgebraicEndpointImages` variant and predicates are replaced by `RetainedBezier`, `is_retained_bezier` and `has_retained_beziers`, describing represented as well as algebraic source ranges without another representation. A forward unit range can stay native; a reversed unit range must retain its chart. Carrier diagnostics print provenance and family without recursively dumping the entire shared curve into each contact.
