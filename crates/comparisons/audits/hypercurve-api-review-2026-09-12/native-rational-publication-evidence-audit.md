This step continues the original active architecture goal after `32ec377`; it does not narrow that goal.

The preserved public baseline demonstrates two completeness defects. For x=4t(1-t), y=0, the native whole-curve report claimed completeness with one identity overlap. Restricting its evidence to t in [0,1/4] and u in [3/4,1] returned no overlap, while a fresh intersection of those pieces returned one. A nodal cubic x=12(2t-1)^2, y=12((2t-1)^3-(2t-1)/4) similarly claimed completeness while omitting the ordered transverse contacts (1/4,3/4) and (3/4,1/4). Both defects occurred under STRICT and APPROXIMATE_512. Baseline source, executable, library hash and output are in `native-rational-publication-baseline.*`.
