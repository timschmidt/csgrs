Preserve endpoint ownership when certifying algebraic images

A closed polynomial or rational image enclosure could be treated as a
half-open root isolator, excluding its actual lower root and selecting a
foreign eliminant root. Separate enclosure bounds and endpoint ownership
from certified IsolatedRootInterval evidence.

Count each owned endpoint strictly before admitting an image. Preserve
orientation for monotone maps, use closed bounds for interval arithmetic,
canonicalize owned endpoint roots, and refine away excluded upper roots
before returning a half-open isolator. Migrate internal callers directly;
remove the obsolete sorting helper and one-sided ownership enum.

Cover polynomial and rational zero images previously reported positive,
reversed maps, repeated roots, each endpoint convention, and exact points.
