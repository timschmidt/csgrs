Exclude projective poles from affine conic intersection contacts

Implicit substitution can have roots at infinity when rational Bezier
weights have mixed signs. Reject zero source denominators before contact
transport and point publication. Reuse common-sign Bernstein weights on
the unit domain, and replay mixed weights strictly in the original source.

A public counterexample with weights [1,-2,1] previously published poles
as transverse contacts. The regression checks complete finite contact
counts and exact coordinates, rational and irrational poles, and both
policies. Rational double-pole cases now return complete empty contact sets.

Validation: 169 release cases/eight pinned binaries; seven build, Clippy,
format, fuzz, documentation and downstream checks. The unchanged public
12-case probe retains 24 finite controls, with zero pole contacts and no
incomplete results (previously eight pole contacts and four incomplete sets).
