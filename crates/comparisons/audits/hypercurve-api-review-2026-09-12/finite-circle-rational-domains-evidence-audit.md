# Finite circle/rational parameter domains

Status: committed as `df5e6a9a7376edb8106bde381dbbbdcdbe01d9ed` and qualified against parent `5c47fb199ddcd687ef030811221acdf76db8a624`. The original architecture goal remains active.

The private `unit_weight_sign()` interface is removed. `denominator_sign(range)` proves the sign on the requested exact closed range; the existing cache holds only a proof for the complete native unit chart. All controlled callers pass their actual domain or explicitly request the authored unit interval. Polynomial and selected-root authorities accept arbitrary exact coefficients and bounds. A pole outside the active range cannot reject a finite curve, and a subrange proof cannot contaminate the whole-unit cache.

Circle/rational discovery and exact angular inverse replay use explicit finite domains across one-field, selected parallel-normal, pair-radial, recursive, represented-frame and chord-normal systems. The common pair dispatcher removes its nonlinear rational unit-domain guard. Native hull and short-conic shortcuts retain their mathematical premises. Components and isolated contacts share a pole-free represented enclosure; original exact bounds remain the consuming pair's clipping authority. Selected bounds are separated from excluded poles without global scalar reconstruction. The selected-fiber path reuses its retained authority, and angular inversion shares the finite root machinery instead of a separate unit isolator. Chord-normal and non-Cartesian represented-frame inversion reuse the existing expression backends. No public carrier or compatibility interface is added.
