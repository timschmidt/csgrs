# Third-order branch ordering: speed and theorem domain

At a regular endpoint with v=C^(1)(0), let e=v/|v| and let x be distance along e. If cross(v,C^(2)(0))=0, the normal-coordinate third derivative with respect to x is cross(v,C^(3)(0))/|v|^4. The terms involving tangential acceleration vanish only under that curvature hypothesis. Its squared magnitude therefore has denominator |v|^8=(v dot v)^4. Under a positive change of parameter with derivative s, the numerator cross(v,C^(3)) scales by s^4 and its square by s^8, matching this denominator. Using (v dot v)^2 does not preserve the local graph.

The public V860 probe supplies exact rational jets for (t,t^3), (2u,8u^3), (2u,4u^3), and (u,2u^3). The first pair has one graph; the next has graph y=x^3/2; the last has graph y=2x^3. Both policies incorrectly order the identical pair and reverse the lower-branch comparison. Six requests finish normally, with four exact oracle mismatches. V862 replays the unchanged source against the candidate library and requires zero mismatches.

The retained second-order comparison uses SameDirection for several insufficient witnesses, including equal nonzero curvature. It must not interpret every such result as the zero-curvature hypothesis. For C_a(u)=(u+a*u^2,(u+a*u^2)^2), a=1 and a=2 describe the same local parabola. Their jets are v=(1,0), a=(2a,2), j=(0,12a): identical nonzero curvature but different raw third-order cross products. The new guard requires both saved curvature signs to be exactly zero before the existing third-order fallback. Missing or equal nonzero curvature remains unresolved; no same-curve certificate is inferred from SameDirection.

The three regressions cover both policies, native and represented arithmetic, every pair of positive integer speeds 1/2/3 and equal/unequal positive cubic graph coefficients. A separate retained-caller test checks the parabola and confirms that the fallback still orders genuinely different cubic departures when both curvatures vanish. Existing endpoint construction, root arithmetic, scalar sign authority, first-order ordering and curvature normalization remain unchanged. These predicate fixes do not establish universal region closure or measured performance improvement.
