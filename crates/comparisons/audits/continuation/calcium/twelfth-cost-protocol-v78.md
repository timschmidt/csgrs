# Checkpoint 78: matched native public-predicate costs

The checkpoint-77 candidate and current live baseline are immutable inputs.
No new source copies or production edits. This campaign is not a retention
decision; independent correctness from 76/77 remains separate from cost evidence.

96 authored cases: 32 twelfth-turn equalities, their 32 rational perturbations,
12 already-supported angles, eight periodic structural equivalents, eight
non-target controls, two deep trigonometric identities, and two large-scaled
twelfth-turn identities. Truth follows exact angle identities, sin^2+cos^2=1,
exp(x+y)=exp(x)exp(y), positive radical identities, or rational comparison.
Unknown is acceptable for an unsupported exact identity, never inequality.

Every case uses -64/-256 precision budgets and three lifecycles (576 groups):

- fresh: construct and drop each pair inside timing; process/global constants
  already warmed, so this is not cold process startup;
- cold: first predicate on each separately preconstructed pair; vector/pair
  construction and final pair destruction are outside timing;
- retained: repeated predicates on the same explicitly warmed pair.

Four preflight warmup queries occur outside timing. Public constructors may
share global constants even on a fresh/cold route. Timed loops consume result
categories through a three-counter histogram. Full preflight/postflight
certificates are recorded where useful, but legitimate proof-route strengthening
must not be confused with a numerical misdecision. No approximate-equality
shortcut or acceptance of equal fingerprints as a mathematical oracle.

CPU and allocation binaries differ only by the qualified counting allocator.
Confirm allocator symbols are absent from CPU binaries. Serialize the campaign,
pin to CPU 2, capture before/between/after host snapshots, and do not overlap
timing with Memcheck or another task-owned benchmark. Four warmups; two pilot
rounds at 1/16 iterations; common calibrated iterations target 2 ms using the
slower per-query pilot, capped at 2,000 for bounded cold-pair memory. Twenty-four
balanced randomized paired CPU rounds with identical shuffled group order per
pair; four allocation pairs at both 1 and 16 iterations. Preserve every sample.

Use the corrected AES/rejection-sampled paired median bootstrap and an independent
order-statistic median interval. Intervals are conditional on sampling assumptions,
not multiplicity-adjusted, and host snapshots do not prove an idle/reserved CPU.
Separate Unknown-to-Equal cases from same-result comparisons. Report slower
bypasses, changed certificate routes, retained caches, allocation requests/bytes,
live deltas and peak above starting live bytes without calling that peak RSS.

Freeze four small executables because identical benchmark crate/bin names keep
source/layout differences controlled; candidate build would otherwise overwrite
the baseline binaries in the shared target. Use one narrowly scoped temporary
directory. No cleanup or duplicate stack copies. WASM runtime/cost, downstream,
representative linked-size and remaining audit references stay open.
