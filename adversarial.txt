Adversarial testing guide for csgrs
===================================

Scope
-----

CSGRS is a modeling grammar over native Hyper geometry. Solid operations take
and return `hypermesh::TriangleMesh`; planar operations take and return
`hypercurve::CurveRegion2`, `CurveString2`, and `CurvePath2`. Hypermesh owns
mesh topology and Boolean provenance. Hypercurve owns planar topology. This
guide deliberately does not define a second mesh, polygon, vertex, plane, or
filled-region carrier.

Required invariants
-------------------

- Finite public input must not produce non-finite positions, normals, bounds,
  areas, volumes, or indices.
- Every triangle index must address the returned position buffer.
- Identity transforms preserve geometry; inverse twice restores orientation.
- Bounds enclose all positions after construction, transforms, Booleans,
  subdivision, implicit generation, and imports.
- Boolean identities hold for empty and identical operands.
- Closed-manifold inputs either produce a valid result or a typed diagnostic;
  they must not hang, panic unexpectedly, or allocate without a configured
  bound.
- Hypercurve region operations preserve classified material and hole loops or
  return explicit uncertainty.
- `AttributedMesh<M>` keeps exactly one metadata row per triangle and, when
  present, one authored normal per position.
- I/O and scalar adapters are lossy only at their documented boundaries.
- Repeated serialized runs are deterministic for geometry and checksums.

Active deterministic suites
---------------------------

Run:

    scripts/adversarial.sh

The script executes the ordinary adversarial integrations, feature matrices,
and optionally every maintained fuzz target. `tests/competitive.rs` is also an
active hard-test surface. The original full-resolution fixture (5,687
positions and 11,894 triangles) must remain active at both Hypermesh and CSGRS
levels; do not replace it with a reduced proxy.

Solid grammar
-------------

Exercise every constructor in `csgrs::solid`, including zero, negative,
extreme, and minimally tessellated parameters. Compose constructors with
translation, rotation, nonuniform and negative scaling, reflection about
`hypermesh::Plane`, inversion, subdivision, convex hull, Minkowski sum,
distribution, flattening, slicing, lofting, and mass-property queries.

For union, difference, intersection, and xor, cover disjoint, contained,
identical, tangent, coplanar, nearly coplanar, sliver, disconnected, inverted,
open, duplicate-triangle, and invalid-index inputs. Audit native Hypermesh
facts and Boolean provenance where the operation exposes them.

Planar grammar
--------------

Exercise every constructor in `csgrs::curve`, including degenerate control
points, repeated points, minimal segment counts, nested holes, touching loops,
self-crossing input, extreme offsets, and empty regions. Cover Boolean
operations, affine transforms, triangulation, extrusion, vector extrusion,
partial and full revolution, path sweep, twisted extrusion, raster tracing,
font outlines, Hershey strings, and planar metaballs.

The curve-to-solid operations must be checked for index validity, cap
orientation, side-wall continuity, hole preservation, and closed-manifold
output when their documented preconditions are satisfied.

Implicit geometry
-----------------

Exercise SDF expressions, TPMS surfaces and solids, and metaballs with empty
domains, singular bounds, tiny grids, maximum configured grids, negative
iso-values, coincident sources, and expressions that return non-finite values.
Diagnostics must explain rejected or clamped work.

Formats and bindings
--------------------

Fuzz every enabled parser with empty, truncated, malformed, oversized, and
mixed-validity inputs. Round-trip representative native meshes through STL,
OBJ, DXF, glTF, VRML, PLY, and AMF where supported, and representative native
regions through SVG and Gerber. Hostile object names and comments must remain
escaped and syntactically valid.

Compile the C header, C++ wrapper, Go wrapper when Go is available, Python
module, strict TypeScript facade, and generated wasm TypeScript declarations.
JavaScript arrays must reject incomplete triples, out-of-range indices, and
non-finite coordinates before native geometry is created.

Performance and resource gates
------------------------------

The serialized competitive suite is the authority for performance changes.
Compare identical workloads, temperatures, sample counts, feature matrices,
and native competitors. A statistically or materially significant regression,
loss of competitive standing, checksum change, or removal of the
full-resolution hard case blocks release.

Use bounded property-test case counts in ordinary CI and longer fuzz campaigns
outside the fast path. Promote every minimized, license-clean failure to a
deterministic regression fixture. Keep third-party or provenance-uncertain
crash artifacts private.
